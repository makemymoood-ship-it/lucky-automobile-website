Our Story

Lucky Automobile & Workshop was founded in 2022 by Babban Ram, starting out as a small workshop in Baksha, Jaunpur. What began as a one-man operation focused on honest, careful car repair has grown into a workshop trusted by 500+ car owners across Jaunpur district.

Every vehicle that comes through our gate is treated the same way — with transparent pricing, genuine parts and a mechanic who explains exactly what's wrong before touching a single bolt.

Lucky Automobile & Workshop is a proprietorship firm based in Jaunpur, Uttar Pradesh, owned and operated by Babban Ram. Since 2022, we have been providing trusted car servicing, engine repair, and denting-painting services to our customers.
